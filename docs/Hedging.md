# Hedge

## How to Hedge
1-do nothing
2-early profit (buy back the position before maturity, ensuring  25%, 50 % or 75% of the maximum profit)
3-Roll the position in time (extend the maturity by buying the current position and selling a new one with a later maturity, evaluate at 21 DTE)
4-Roll the position in strike and time (buy the current position and sell a new one with a different strike price and a later maturity)
5-Convert to another product (e.g., convert a put credit spread into an iron condor, by buying an additional call credit spread)
6-Hedge Delta (e.g., for a put credit spread (positive delta), hedge the delta with a long put, short shares, short call, long inverse ETF exposure, or a combination of these)
7-Hedge Vega (e.g., for a put credit spread (negative vega), hedge the vega with a long call, long put, long shares, long call spread, long straddle, long calander spread, or a combination of these)
8-Hedge Gamma (e.g., for a put credit spread (negative gamma), hedge the gamma with a OTM long put, longer DTE)
